Meet the team

Our tutors.

Our tutors bring academic, professional and teaching experience across a wide range of subjects and age groups. The academy is led by founder Saira Iffat Bhatti, whose background in cognitive and clinical neuroscience informs its learner-centred ethos.

IK

Inayah Khan

Medical student · Biology, Chemistry & Maths

A first-year medical student with A grades in A-Level Biology and Maths and strong GCSE results across 12 subjects. Inayah has experience as a tutor and mentor, STEM ambassador and student prefect, alongside healthcare-related work experience and volunteering.

Teaching approach

She combines secure subject knowledge with exam technique, adapting explanations to each student and nurturing enjoyment of learning.

AZ

Anser Zaffar

Commissioning pharmacist · GCSE & A-Level tutor

Anser holds a Master's degree (2017), qualified as a pharmacist in 2018 and completed a postgraduate diploma in clinical pharmacy in 2022. He has taught GCSE and A-Level students since 2014 in person and online.

Teaching approach

He identifies areas of weakness and uses interactive, personalised lessons. He reports that his students often exceed predicted grades by one to two grades.

Shefalee studies dentistry at King's College London. She achieved A* A* A at A-Level and nine grade 9s and one grade 8 at GCSE. Her experience includes school and library volunteering, mentoring, student leadership and dental and pharmacy work experience.

Teaching approach

She brings a strong interest in STEM, student wellbeing and mentoring, and values supportive teaching that builds confidence.
