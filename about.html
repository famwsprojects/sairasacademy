About Saira’s Academy

Thoughtful teaching. Confident learning.

Saira’s Academy was founded by Saira Iffat Bhatti with a simple belief: every learner deserves teaching that recognises their individual strengths, builds understanding and helps them move forward with confidence.

A message from our founder

Learning should make sense — and make a difference.

My academic journey has centred on understanding how people think, learn and remember. I studied Cognitive and Clinical Neuroscience at the University of Westminster, graduating with First-Class Honours, before completing an MSc in Dementia: Causes, Treatments and Research (Neuroscience) at University College London, with Distinction.

That background informs the way I think about education: students benefit when ideas are explained clearly, knowledge is revisited and lessons are shaped around how each individual learns. I founded Saira’s Academy to bring together dedicated tutors and provide supportive, purposeful tuition for learners at different stages of their education.

Saira Iffat Bhatti
Founder, Saira’s Academy

How we approach learning

Evidence-aware, individual and encouraging.

Our approach brings together careful planning, responsive teaching and consistent practice. Saira’s neuroscience education provides a valuable perspective on cognition, memory and learning; our tutors bring their own subject knowledge and experience to each lesson.

✦

Start with the learner

We take time to understand each student’s current knowledge, goals and areas where extra support would be useful.

◎

Build lasting understanding

Clear explanations and strong foundations help students make sense of a subject, rather than simply memorising answers.

↗

Make progress visible

Purposeful practice, feedback and progress tracking help students recognise what they have achieved and what to work on next.
